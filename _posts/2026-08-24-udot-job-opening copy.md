**Transportation Data Analyst**

The UDOT Planning Division is seeking a visionary Data Analyst to revolutionize our transportation insights. In this high-impact role, you will transform complex datasets into clear, actionable solutions that shape Utah's future. You will lead the charge in modernizing our prioritization models and socioeconomic data development processes (for travel demand models), leveraging systems thinking to create innovative, future-proof technical processes. Beyond modeling, you'll have the possibility to drive statewide progress by supporting diverse initiatives, including the long range transportation plan and corridor studies, ensuring your work has a lasting legacy across Utah's transportation landscape.

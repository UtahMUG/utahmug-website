**Position title. Senior Consultant, Travel Modeler**

RSG seeks a talented and versatile travel modeler to develop and apply travel forecasting models, manage projects and staff, and proactively market RSG’s capabilities to a wide range of clients.

As a Senior Consultant specializing in the development and application of forecasting tools and data products, you support US transportation agencies as they navigate the rapidly evolving world of mobility. You contribute to business development and management of travel demand model development and application projects, while playing a critical role in expanding RSG’s presence. You will leverage your extensive network and deep understanding of the transportation sector to drive business growth, develop strategic partnerships, and oversee the successful execution of high-impact projects. This role requires a strategic thinker with a proven technical track record, as well as a passion for improving transportation systems through the development and application of state of the practice and advanced travel demand models.
